Add real notices from the official website

The official site does not allow automatic downloads, so you copy a notice yourself (this is normal use of a public page):

  1. Open the official Southern Railway press-release page, choose Chennai under "Press Release - Divisions", and click a notice such as "Changes in the pattern of Train Services".
  2. Select all the notice text (Ctrl+A), copy it (Ctrl+C) and paste it into the box below. Paste the notice's web address in the first box.

Or upload a saved PDF

Importing the same notice twice does nothing. A revised notice with the same number updates the existing one and keeps its history.

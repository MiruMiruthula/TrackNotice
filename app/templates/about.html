About TransitNotice & its limits

Who is it for?

  • Daily commuter (e.g. a college student on bus routes 12B / 21G): wants to know before leaving home whether a diversion or outage affects the route, and for how long.
  • Wheelchair-using metro rider / caregiver: needs advance notice of station or stop closures and shuttle arrangements to plan an accessible journey.
  • Community help-desk volunteer: answers passengers' questions and needs one searchable page with the official source and the last verified time.

What it does

It reads only public advisories from sources that permit automated access (robots.txt, terms and rate limits respected), extracts affected routes, validity window and change type, and keeps a history of every revision.

Limitations - please read

  • It is scheduled monitoring, not real time. A notice can be published minutes before our next check.
  • If the agency does not publish a notice online, or publishes it only as an image/PDF or on social media, we will not see it.
  • Parsing depends on the page layout; if the layout changes, extraction may fail. When that happens the app shows a source error rather than pretending data is live.
  • An advisory with no announced end time is shown as active, end not announced; it can end at any time.
  • Alerts are best-effort. Never rely on TransitNotice alone for safety-critical or time-critical decisions.
